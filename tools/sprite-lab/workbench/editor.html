Pick the look that reads best

Pixel art is chunky and retro; smaller sizes hide sketchy detail. HD keeps your drawing's own lines and shading. Your marked parts carry over if you switch later under Sprite.

Smoothness

Higher fps adds in-between frames, so each motion keeps its speed and just moves more smoothly. 12 fps is the classic pixel-art look and keeps sheets small.

Intensity makes every move bigger or smaller. More frames give smoother motion and a longer sheet. Shading adds a shadow band on each part's lower back edge and darkens the far arm and leg.
